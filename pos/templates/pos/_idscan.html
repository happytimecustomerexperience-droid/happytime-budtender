{% comment %} The ID scanner. ONE copy, included by the station and by the door. It exists as an include because the two had drifted: the station got a live camera with a big preview and the door — the screen whose entire job is scanning IDs — was still hiding the camera behind a "Scan barcode on device" button. Two copies of the same widget is how that happens. Everything decodes IN THE BROWSER: only the decoded barcode text is posted, never a picture of someone's licence. The guide is ONE orientation-agnostic bracket box. AAMVA D.11.1 puts the PDF417 "perpendicular to the natural bottom of the card", which for a portrait card implies a rotated symbol — unconfirmed for Washington, and zxing finds rotated symbols anyway. A guide that insisted on an orientation would be wrong half the time. Deliberately NOT labelled "vertical = under 21": a WA vertical card runs its full 8-year term (RCW 46.20.181), so one can sit in a 27-year-old's wallet. DOB decides. Caller passes `dom_id`. {% endcomment %}
